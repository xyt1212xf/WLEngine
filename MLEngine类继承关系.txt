一、完整继承树
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

【根基类】(无父类)
CRefcount (引用计数基类)
CNonCopyable (禁拷贝基类)
CDeviceBase (设备抽象基类)
CWinPlatform (Windows 平台层)
CGameApp    (应用入口)
CRenderer   (渲染器)
CResourceFactory (资源工厂)
CResourceMgr     (资源管理器)
CVertexStreamFactory (abstract) (顶点流工厂)
CMeshAttributes  (烘焙数据)
CStaticMeshLODResources (LOD 渲染数据)
CThreadPool      (线程池)
FMatrix44(矩阵)
FViewMatrices(试图矩阵)
TVec2(向量)
TVec3(向量)
TVec4(向量)

【CRefcount 系】(资源与对象生命周期)
CRefcount
├── CObject                    (带名字的对象, UE UObject 对标)
│   ├── CStreamableRenderAsset (可流送渲染资产, UE UStreamableRenderAsset 对标)
│   ├── CActor                 (演员, UE AActor 对标)
│   │   └── CCharacter         (角色, UE ACharacter 对标)
│   └── (CClass : StructInfo)  (反射类信息, 见 StructInfo 系)
├── CResource                  (资源基类, 持 UUID/状态/类型)
│   └── CMesh                  (网格资源基类)
│       └── CStaticMesh        (静态网格, UE UStaticMesh 对标)
└── CDX12RHIbuffer             (DX12 缓冲实现, 持 ID3D12Resource)  
    └──CRHIBuffer              (RHI 缓冲抽象)


【StructInfo 系】(反射)
StructInfo (结构体信息: 类名/父类指针/继承缓存)
└── CClass    (运行时类信息: IsChildOf 查询)


【CDeviceBase 系】(设备)
CDeviceBase
└── CDX12RHIDevice  (DX12 设备: SwapChain/CommandQueue/RTV/Fence)


【CPlug 系】(插件, 单例)
CPlug : TSingle<CPlug>          (引擎插件接口)
└── CGraphicPlug                (图形插件: 持 Device + Renderer)


【CEngine】(引擎主类)
CEngine : TSingle<CEngine>      (引擎单例: 持 Plug 数组/线程池/平台层)


【CComponent 系】(组件)
CComponent : CNonCopyable       (组件基类: tick/名字)
├── CTransformComponent         (变换组件, UE USceneComponent 对标)
└── CStaticMeshComponent        (静态网格组件, UE UStaticMeshComponent 对标)


【CMeshAttributes 系】(烘焙中间数据)
CMeshAttributes                 (平铺顶点流 + Sections + BakeFromDescription)
└── CStaticMeshAttributes       (当前为空, 预留扩展)


【CVertexStreamFactory 系】(顶点工厂)
CVertexStreamFactory (abstract) (BuildInputLayout 纯虚)
└── CStaticVertexFactory        (静态网格顶点流, UE FLocalVertexFactory 对标)

[CWorld系]
CWorld
└── CResource        
CLevle
└── CResource
CLevelStreaming
└── CRefcount
CWorldSet
└── CRefcount
      


二、数据结构 (POD struct, 无继承)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FMeshVertex            (拓扑顶点, 只有 Position)
FMeshVertexInstance    (顶点实例: VertexID + Normal/Tangent/UV0/Color)
FMeshTriangle          (三角形: 3 个 VertexInstanceID + PolygonGroupID)
FMeshPolygonGroup      (多边形组/材质槽: Name + MaterialIndex)
FMeshDescription       (拓扑容器: 持上述 4 个数组)
FStaticMeshSection     (可绘制分段: MaterialIndex/FirstIndex/NumTriangles)
FObjectHeader          (对象头: Handle/Size/Name/GC标记)
SDataStreamUnit        (顶点流单元: Buffer/Slot/Offset/Stride/Format)
SMeshVertexSchema      (顶点布局描述: 8 个 SDataStreamUnit)
SInputElement          (输入布局元素)


三、关键组合关系 (持有, 非继承)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CEngine
 ├── [持有] TArray<CPlug*>          → CGraphicPlug
 ├── [持有] CThreadPool*
 └── [持有] CWinPlatform

CGraphicPlug
 ├── [持有] CDX12RHIDevice*
 └── [持有] CRenderer*

CRenderer
 └── [持有] CGraphicPlug*           (渲染线程通过它拿设备)

CDX12RHIDevice
 └── [持有] ID3D12Device/CommandQueue/SwapChain/RTV Heap/Fence...

CStaticMesh                          (UE UStaticMesh)
 ├── [持有] TArray<CStaticMeshDescription*>  SourceModels  (每 LOD 源数据)
 ├── [持有] TArray<CStaticMeshLODResources*> RenderData    (每 LOD 渲染数据)
 └── [持有] TArray<std::string>      MaterialSlots         (材质槽名)

CStaticMeshDescription               (UE FStaticMeshSourceModel)
 └── [持有] FMeshDescription         OwnedMeshDescription  (拓扑数据)

CStaticMeshLODResources              (UE FStaticMeshLODResources)
 ├── [持有] CRHIBuffer* ×4           Position/Tangent/Color/IndexBuffer
 └── [持有] TArray<FStaticMeshSection> Sections

CStaticMeshComponent
 └── [引用] CStaticMesh*             (组件引用网格, 不持有)

CDX12RHIbuffer
 └── [持有] ID3D12Resource*


四、静态网格数据流 (P0 已打通)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CStaticMeshDescription::CreateCube()
    │ 填充
    ▼
FMeshDescription (拓扑: Vertex/VertexInstance/Triangle/PolygonGroup)
    │ CMeshAttributes::BakeFromDescription()  平铺+按材质分组
    ▼
CMeshAttributes (平铺流: Positions/Normals/Tangents/UVs/Indices + Sections)
    │ CStaticMeshLODResources::InitResources()  拷贝元数据+算包围球
    ▼
CStaticMeshLODResources (Sections + Bounds + Buffer指针[P1填GPU])
    │ P1: VB/IB 上传
    ▼
GPU (per-stream vertex buffers + index buffer)
